Privacy Policy

Right Foot · Effective 28 September 2026

Right Foot collects no data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no third-party code, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

  • Your alarms and settings.
  • Your codes: the value read from each barcode or QR code and the name you give it. Never a photo.
  • A history of your alarms, used to count emergency stops.

This data is stored only on your device. It is not synced to iCloud, but like any app data it is part of your device backups. Deleting the app deletes it.

Camera

The camera reads codes on your device. Images are never saved or sent anywhere.

Screen Time

If you choose apps or websites to block, iOS gives Right Foot only anonymous tokens. Right Foot cannot see which apps you picked or how you use them.

Notifications

Awake check uses local notifications, scheduled on your device.

Feedback

If you send feedback, it goes through your own mail app as a normal email. It contains your message, any screenshots you add, the app version and your device model and iOS version, plus your email address. It is used only to reply and to improve the app, and it is never shared. Ask and it will be deleted.
